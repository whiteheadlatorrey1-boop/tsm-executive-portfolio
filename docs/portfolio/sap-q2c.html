How it works, end to end

A walkthrough of the daily flow

  1. Process analyst

    Master data and catalog

    Start at the front of the chain. Master data and the catalog show a duplicate customer and a product missing its pricing condition.

  2. Process analyst

    Downstream impact

    Trace downstream. The same records sit behind two open opportunities, one live quote and one order on hold.

  3. Process analyst

    Handoffs and controls

    Integration Hub shows which handoff failed, and Governance confirms the approval controls on the quote.

  4. Sales operations

    Strategist

    The strategist ranks the fix: correct the source record once, and every downstream stage clears.

  5. Executive

    Executive handoff

    The executive handoff goes to the enterprise digital twin, with the sample evidence attached and labeled.
