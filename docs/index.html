Questions

If yours isn't here, ask on the call.

Do you replace my team?

No. We take the repetitive first pass and the checking off their plate, so your people spend their time on the decisions only they can make.

Is the data in your demos real?

No. Every demo uses synthetic data. Your own files are used only during a pilot or service you have signed for.

What happens in week 1 of a pilot?

We set up your accounts and run a small, agreed sample of your real files through the app. If anything doesn't fit your formats, we fix it before the weekly runs start.

Can you work with patient health information?

Only after a business associate agreement is signed and the required safeguards are in place. Until then, healthcare pilots use de-identified or synthetic samples.

What if the pilot doesn't pay for itself?

You'll know, because we measure it with you every week. Either side can end the pilot with five business days' notice.
