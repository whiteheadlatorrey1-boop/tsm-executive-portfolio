How much revenue is sitting in denied and unworked claims at your practice right now?

Meet Healthcare Denial. Governed denial triage: denials entered or imported, grouped by category and aging, reviewed and published as a client report.

Here is the problem today. Denials pile up faster than the team can work them. Every payer has different rules and deadlines. Leaders cannot see which issues cost the most.

Step 1. Sign in as analyst and enter a denial or import the denial CSV.

Step 2. Check the grouping by category and aging.

Step 3. Read the appeal-deadline flag; it always needs human review.

Step 4. Submit for independent review.

Step 5. As reviewer, confirm or correct; corrections create a new version.

Step 6. As publisher, approve and publish.

Step 7. As client, refresh and open the report and PDF.

Here is what your team gets: Analyst intake or denial-file CSV import, Grouping by denial category and aging, and more, all in one workspace.

The business value. Work the denial backlog in priority order. Fewer missed appeal deadlines. Consistent documentation for every appeal decision.

People stay in charge. Every result is reviewed and approved by a person before it is released, every change is versioned, and every action is on the audit trail.
