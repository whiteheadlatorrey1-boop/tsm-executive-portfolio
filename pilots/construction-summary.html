Construction BPO

Governed back-office review for general contractors: job-cost imports, pay apps, invoices and lien waivers checked, reviewed and reported.

Request a pilot▶ Watch the demo
THE PROBLEM

Job cost and billing data live in separate spreadsheets.

Underbilling and profit fade are found too late to fix.

Nobody can prove who reviewed the numbers before the client saw them.

THE VALUE

Catch lien-waiver and pay-app mismatches before money goes out

Fewer month-end job-cost surprises

Back-office capacity without adding headcount

What it does

  • Job-cost and invoice import
  • Pay application and lien-waiver cross-checks
  • Financial exception flags with unassessed items listed
  • Independent review and correction versioning
  • Approved client report with matching branded PDF
  • Weekly-review Copilot (read-only) and full audit trail
