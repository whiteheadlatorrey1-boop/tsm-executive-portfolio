Meet Mortgage Servicing Compliance. Compliance-exception triage for mortgage servicing teams: loan-file exceptions are flagged, prioritized and routed for human review.

Here is the problem today. QC exceptions are tracked in spreadsheets. High-severity issues wait in the same line as minor ones. QC reports are rebuilt by hand every cycle.

Step 1. Load the day's loan-file exceptions.

Step 2. Work the severity-ranked queue.

Step 3. Route each exception to a reviewer.

Step 6. As approver, publish it and check the PDF.

Here is what your team gets: Loan-file exception intake, Severity-ranked exception queue, and more, all in one workspace.

The business value. Exceptions worked in order of severity. Nothing aging silently in the queue. A defensible QC record for every loan.

People stay in charge. Every result is reviewed and approved by a person before it is released, every change is versioned, and every action is on the audit trail.

IF THEY ASK

Is the data real? No. Every demo record is synthetic.

Who has the final say? Your reviewer; nothing is released without a person approving it.

Limits: Flags exceptions for human review only; no RESPA, TILA or fair-lending determinations.

Limits: No underwriting or credit decisions.
