Capabilities

What your team gets

  • 8-step Song Builder with a live 'Your song so far' sheet
  • Co-write: three ideas per step (titles, story, hook, tempo and key, structure, verses, bridge)
  • Encouragement line after every step, based on what the artist wrote
  • Complete sample song for guided demos

How it works, end to end

A walkthrough of the daily flow

  1. Artist

    Your latest song

    Sign in as the artist; the latest saved song opens.

  2. Artist

    Co-write

    Walk the 8 steps; use Co-write when stuck and Keep, Edit or Try again.

  3. Artist

    Your song so far

    Watch the song build in 'Your song so far'.

  4. Artist

    Coach my draft

    Open Coach my draft to plan delivery over the practice beat.

  5. Artist

    Take Sheet

    Save, then print the song or Take Sheet for the session.

Safeguards

People stay in charge

The artist keeps, edits or replaces every idea; Co-write writes original lines only.

BOUNDARIES

Original lines only: no named-artist imitation or lyric copying.

Drafts are for the artist's own use; no music licensing or distribution claims.

Synthetic or owner-supplied audio only.
