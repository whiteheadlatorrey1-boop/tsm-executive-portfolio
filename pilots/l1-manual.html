Running ServiceNow L1 Workbench

How each role uses the app every day, step by step.

Roles

RoleWhat they do
TechnicianWorks the incident queue and reviews drafts
ReviewerApproves categorization, priority and response
PublisherReleases the technician handoff
ClientReads approved handoffs

Daily flow

  1. Technician

    Today's tickets

    Load the day's exported or sample tickets.

  2. Technician

    Drafted triage

    Open each incident and read the drafted category, priority and first response.

  3. Technician

    Correction

    Correct anything wrong; corrections are versioned.

  4. Technician

    Submit for review

    Submit for review.

  5. Lead

    Reviewer

    As reviewer, approve or return each draft.

  6. Lead

    Technician handoff

    Publish the technician handoff and check the PDF.

BEFORE YOU START

Sign in with your own pilot account; never share accounts.

Use only synthetic or approved sample data during the pilot.

Keep the app's gate and account passwords out of email and chat.

BOUNDARIES

No writes to a live ServiceNow instance until the client approves an integration.

Pilot uses exported or synthetic tickets only.
