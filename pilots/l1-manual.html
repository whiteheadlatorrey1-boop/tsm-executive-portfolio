Running ServiceNow L1 Workbench

Pilot: tsm-l1-pilot-tsm-l1-pilot.up.railway.app

Before you start

  • Get your access-gate login and your role account from the owner. Never share them or paste them into email or chat.
  • Open the pilot link. Enter the gate username and password in the browser pop-up, then sign in with your role account.
  • Use synthetic or owner-supplied data only.

Roles

RoleWhat they do
TechnicianWorks the incident queue and reviews drafts
ReviewerApproves categorization, priority and response
PublisherReleases the technician handoff
ClientReads approved handoffs

A different person should review than prepared the work. When the owner is the only operator, the system records the reason code OWNER_SOLE_OPERATOR and discloses it on the report.

Daily workflow

  1. Load the day's exported or sample tickets.
  2. Open each incident and read the drafted category, priority and first response.
  3. Correct anything wrong; corrections are versioned.
  4. Submit for review.
  5. As reviewer, approve or return each draft.
  6. Publish the technician handoff and check the PDF.

Weekly review

  • Open the weekly-review Copilot where available. It is read-only and never approves or publishes.
  • Check open items, returned work and anything listed as unassessed.
  • Use View audit events to confirm who did what.

Limits you must respect

  • No writes to a live ServiceNow instance until the client approves an integration.
  • Pilot uses exported or synthetic tickets only.

Troubleshooting

  • Gate pop-up keeps returning: the gate login was mistyped. Copy it again with nothing before or after it.
  • Sent back to the sign-in page: the account password was wrong. Several failed tries lock the account briefly; wait a few minutes or use another role.
  • Client sees no reports: nothing has been published yet. Clients only see approved, published reports.
  • Anything else: contact the owner at torreyw94@tsmatter.com.
