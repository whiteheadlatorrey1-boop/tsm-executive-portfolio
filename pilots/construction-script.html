How many days does your team lose every month reconciling job costs before the WIP report goes out?

Meet Construction BPO. Governed back-office review for general contractors: job-cost imports, pay apps, invoices and lien waivers checked, reviewed and reported.

Here is the problem today. Job cost and billing data live in separate spreadsheets. Underbilling and profit fade are found too late to fix. Nobody can prove who reviewed the numbers before the client saw them.

Step 1. Import the job-cost or invoice package for the job.

Step 2. Run the analysis and read every financial exception it raises.

Step 3. Record anything the system could not assess under Unassessed items.

Step 4. Submit for independent review.

Step 5. As reviewer, confirm or correct each finding; corrections create a new version.

Step 6. As approver, approve and publish the client report.

Step 7. Confirm the client report and PDF match before notifying the client.

Here is what your team gets: Job-cost and invoice import, Pay application and lien-waiver cross-checks, and more, all in one workspace.

The business value. Catch lien-waiver and pay-app mismatches before money goes out. Fewer month-end job-cost surprises. Back-office capacity without adding headcount.

People stay in charge. Every result is reviewed and approved by a person before it is released, every change is versioned, and every action is on the audit trail.

See Construction BPO on your own work. Request a pilot at torreyw94@tsmatter.com.
