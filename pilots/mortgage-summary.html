Executive summary · Mortgage servicing

Mortgage Servicing Compliance

Status: in buildPilot pricing on request

What it does

Compliance-exception triage for mortgage servicing teams: loan-file exceptions are flagged, prioritized and routed for human review.

  • Loan-file exception intake
  • Severity-ranked exception queue
  • Routing to the right reviewer
  • Unassessed items listed
  • Approved QC report with PDF
  • Audit trail and read-only Copilot

Business outcomes

  • Exceptions worked in order of severity
  • Nothing aging silently in the queue
  • A defensible QC record for every loan

Who it is for

Mortgage servicing compliance and QC managers, subservicers, credit unions with servicing portfolios

Safeguards and limits

  • Synthetic or owner-supplied data only during the pilot; no live client records.
  • Every result goes through independent human review and approval before a client sees it.
  • Full audit trail of intake, review, corrections, approval and publishing.
  • Each pilot runs behind an access gate, with separate tenants and role-based accounts.
  • Flags exceptions for human review only; no RESPA, TILA or fair-lending determinations.
  • No underwriting or credit decisions.
