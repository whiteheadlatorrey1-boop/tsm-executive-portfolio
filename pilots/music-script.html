What if every new artist had a co-writer by their side the moment they started their first song?

Meet Music. A songwriting workspace with a co-writer by the artist's side: an 8-step wizard, three ideas at every step to keep, edit or replace, the song building live on screen, encouragement after each step, and a printable Take Sheet for the first recording.

Here is the problem today. New artists freeze in front of a blank page. Lessons end, and practice between sessions has no structure. Students reach the studio without a finished draft.

Step 1. Sign in as the artist; the latest saved song opens.

Step 2. Walk the 8 steps; use Co-write when stuck and Keep, Edit or Try again.

Step 3. Watch the song build in 'Your song so far'.

Step 4. Open Coach my draft to plan delivery over the practice beat.

Step 5. Save, then print the song or Take Sheet for the session.

Here is what your team gets: 8-step Song Builder with a live 'Your song so far' sheet, Co-write: three ideas per step (titles, story, hook, tempo and key, structure, verses, bridge), and more, all in one workspace.

The business value. New artists stop staring at a blank page. Confidence grows because every kept line is the artist's choice. Students arrive at the studio with a finished draft ready to record.

The artist stays in charge. Every idea is theirs to keep, edit or replace, Co-write writes original lines only, and the song always belongs to the artist.
