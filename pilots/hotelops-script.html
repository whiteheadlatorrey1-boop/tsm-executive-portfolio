How many housekeeping and maintenance tickets at your hotel are about to miss their promised time?

Meet HotelOps. Housekeeping and maintenance work orders across every room: severity-based SLAs, assignment, resolution and a full audit trail for hotel operations teams.

Here is the problem today. Tickets live on radios, notes and whiteboards. At-risk requests are noticed only after a guest complains. Daily operations reporting is rebuilt by hand every morning.

Step 1. Log new housekeeping and maintenance tickets by room.

Step 2. Check the at-risk and breached SLA list first.

Step 3. Assign each open ticket to staff with a due time.

Step 4. Mark resolutions as staff report back.

Step 5. Submit the daily operations report for review.

Step 6. As approver, publish it and check the PDF.

Here is what your team gets: Room-level housekeeping and maintenance ticket intake, Severity-based SLA timers per ticket type, and more, all in one workspace.

The business value. No out-of-order room sits unnoticed. SLA breaches seen before guests feel them. One record of who fixed what, and when.

People stay in charge. Every result is reviewed and approved by a person before it is released, every change is versioned, and every action is on the audit trail.

See HotelOps on your own work. Request a pilot at torreyw94@tsmatter.com.

IF THEY ASK

Is the data real? No. Every demo record is synthetic.

Who has the final say? Your reviewer; nothing is released without a person approving it.

Limits: Operations tracking only; no revenue, RevPAR or occupancy projections.

Limits: No guest payment or personal data is processed in the pilot.
